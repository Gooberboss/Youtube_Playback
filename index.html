Record your screen

Record a screen, window, or browser tab straight into your library. Keeps recording while you use other apps.

This browser can’t record the screen. Screen recording works in Edge or Chrome on a computer. On iPhone, use iOS’s built-in Screen Recording, then import the video above.

To keep recording while you use other apps, share your entire screen or the window/tab you’ll be using, then leave this app open (minimizing is fine). Closing this app ends the recording. Only record content you have the right to keep; this isn’t for saving other people’s YouTube videos.
